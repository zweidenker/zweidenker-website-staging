Innovation process

How an idea becomes a product

1 · Understanding

Analysis of your processes, effort estimate, concept — including when you have no solution in mind yet, only a goal.

2 · Ideation

Creativity needs structure or the flood of ideas turns chaotic. With analogy and association techniques we generate ideas deliberately rather than by chance.

3 · Delivery

The idea becomes software you can test early. A multi-day innovation sprint shows whether a plan holds — before it gets expensive.
