App development

An app agency in Cologne — since 2009

We specialise in apps that are scalable, business-critical or close to the hardware, and that have to meet serious security requirements. From start-up to corporate, for iOS, Android and Flutter.

  • Native apps for iOS and Android, cross-platform with Flutter
  • Professional UX/UI design with clickable prototypes
  • Backends, IoT integration and operations from one team

What we build

We have been designing and building bespoke apps since 2009, covering the whole chain — ideation and UX design, front end and back end, operations and maintenance.

Our strength is applications that have to hold up: car sharing for millions of users, a ticketing backend calculating prices on the fly, a surgery manager running in a clinic.

Why it is faster with us

Because we do not start from zero. Fifteen years of project work produced ApptiveGrid — our own platform for the data, forms, approvals and reporting that sit behind most apps.

For your project that means the standard parts already exist. We build bespoke where it makes a difference, and reuse where it does not.

Project phases

How a project runs here

Phase 1–3 · Understand

Kick-off workshop, requirements analysis, concept. What comes out is what gets built and why — not just a list of features.

Phase 4–5 · Build

Design sprint, then development in sprints. We launch an MVP first and extend it with updates, rather than building in the dark for a year.

Phase 6 · Operate

After release, maintenance and further development keep the app current — including when iOS and Android move underneath it.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build an app?
It depends on scope and complexity. A minimum viable product takes 1–3 months, an average app 3–9 months. We launch an MVP first and extend it with updates.
What does app development cost?
It depends on the features, the devices to support and a number of other factors. Custom apps typically start in the five figures. If your plan comes down to data, forms and approvals, a solution on ApptiveGrid is usually considerably cheaper and faster — and we will say so in the first conversation.
What is included in the price?
Detailed consulting up front, concept and programming, regular demo versions during implementation, final testing, and publishing to the respective store.
Native app, hybrid app or progressive web app?
It depends on the features and the devices. To bring an existing website to mobile, a progressive web app is often enough. For device-specific functions, offline availability or push notifications, native or Flutter is the right call.
Which platforms do you develop for?
iOS, Android and Flutter on the front end, Pharo on the back end. For cross-platform apps we have relied on Flutter for years — KVV Homezone runs on iOS, Android and web from one codebase.
What do you need from us?
For the first conversation, a description of the problem is enough — no specification required. For a firm quote we work out together which features belong in scope, in a requirements workshop if that helps.
At what point can you help?
From the idea to the finished product. Depending on how far planning has come, we advise on requirements, concept and design — or step straight into delivery.

Let's talk about your project.

Tell us what needs building. We will say honestly what has to be built from scratch and what does not.
