Pharo

Development with Pharo — smart processes, all of a piece

We support the development of new products and business models as well as the rebuild and migration of existing IT infrastructure to Pharo.

Why we work with Pharo

Pharo is a purely object-oriented language and a powerful environment built around simplicity and immediate feedback. For us it stands for a high bar on software quality.

As part of the worldwide Pharo community we help drive it forward — with our own open-source libraries, at conferences, and in exchange with others who use it in production.
