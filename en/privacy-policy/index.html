Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Privacy at a glance

General information

The following notes provide an overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data that can be used to identify you personally.

This is a static website. It consists of pre-generated files that are delivered without a database and without server-side processing. That limits data processing considerably:

  • This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser — neither cookies nor local storage nor session storage. There is therefore no cookie banner.
  • No third-party content is embedded. No maps, no videos, no social media elements. We also serve the typefaces from our own server, so no connection to Google Fonts is established.
  • There is no contact form. You can reach us by email, by phone, or through the linked appointment booking.

Data collection on this website

Simply opening a page automatically records technical access data in our host’s server log files (see section 2) and performs an anonymous audience measurement (see section 5). We receive all other data only because you actively provide it — in an email, for example.

2. Hosting

GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a Microsoft Corporation company).

When you visit this website, GitHub processes the technically necessary access data. This includes in particular:

  • your IP address
  • the date and time of access
  • the file requested
  • the previously visited page (referrer URL)
  • browser type and version, and the operating system used

This data is required in order to deliver the website at all, to ensure its stability and to prevent abuse. We ourselves have no access to these log files and cannot analyse them.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in providing our online offering securely and reliably.

Processing also takes place on servers in the USA. For details of GitHub’s data processing, see GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

4. Data collection on this website

No cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores no data in your browser. No recognition across visits takes place. Consent to the setting of cookies is therefore not required, and none is requested.

Server log files

When the website is accessed, access data is processed in our host’s log files. Which data this is and on what legal basis it is processed is set out in section 2. This data is not merged with other data sources.

Enquiry by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, your enquiry including all resulting personal data — such as name, contact details and the content of the enquiry — will be stored and processed by us for the purpose of handling your request. We do not pass this data on without your consent.

This data is processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry is related to the performance of a contract or is necessary for pre-contractual measures. In all other cases, processing is based on our legitimate interest in effectively handling enquiries addressed to us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) where this has been requested.

The data remains with us until you ask us to delete it, withdraw your consent to storage, or the purpose for storing it no longer applies — for example once your request has been dealt with. Mandatory statutory provisions, in particular retention periods, remain unaffected.

5. Audience measurement

Plausible Analytics

This website uses the web analytics service Plausible Analytics. The provider is Plausible Insights OÜ, Västriku tn 2, 50403 Tartu, Estonia.

Plausible works without cookies and without recognising individuals. No cross-device profile is created, no identifier is stored in your browser, and no information about your visits to other websites is collected. All data is processed and stored on servers within the European Union.

Only aggregated information is collected, which does not allow any conclusions to be drawn about you personally:

  • the page visited
  • the previously visited page (referrer)
  • approximate information about device type, browser and operating system
  • the country from which the access originates

Your IP address is not stored. It is used only briefly to determine the country and to distinguish one visit from another; it is then discarded.

In addition, we record two events: clicks on links leading from this website to other websites — in particular to apptivegrid.de — and requests for pages that do not exist, together with the address requested. The former shows us which content leads to an enquiry; the latter helps us find broken references following the relaunch of this website.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in a data-minimising analysis of how our offering is used, in order to improve it. As no information is stored in or read from your device, no consent under section 25 TDDDG is required.

For further information on data processing by Plausible, see https://plausible.io/data-policy.

6. External services and links

No third-party content is embedded on this website. You reach the following services via links — a connection to their servers is established only when you click the respective link. From that point on, the provider’s own privacy policy applies.

Appointment booking via Google Calendar

To arrange a conversation, we link to a Google Calendar appointment page. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

If you follow this link, Google processes your access data and — if you book an appointment — the details you enter there, such as name, email address and subject. These details are then transmitted to us as a calendar entry and serve solely to prepare and hold the conversation. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out.

The legal basis for processing the booking data on our side is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, as arranging an appointment serves to carry out pre-contractual measures. Opening the appointment page happens at your own initiative.

Further information: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en.

Map link to OpenStreetMap

On the contact page we link our address to OpenStreetMap. The map is not embedded; data is transmitted to the OpenStreetMap Foundation only when you click the link. Further information: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

Other external links

In several places we link to apptivegrid.de, our own product offering, and in the project descriptions to app stores and to our clients’ websites. Each of these websites is subject to its own privacy policy. We have no influence over the content of external pages.
