Speekly

The startup Speekly set out to help brands grow their revenue through user generated content, bringing content creators and brands together on fair terms.

With Speekly we set ourselves the goal of building a modern cross-platform app in Flutter, quickly and iteratively, as the foundation for their content creators. We were alongside them from defining the requirements and shaping the mobile user experience through development and beyond.

Download
App Store
Google Play

Flexible and dependable

The point was to create something genuinely useful for content creators: work made simpler, and communication with brands folded sensibly into how they already work.

Delivery focused on speed, flexibility and a firm cost guarantee, so a young startup could actually plan around the project. Twelve weeks later the finished app was released on both the Play Store and the App Store, beta tests included.

The features

The Speekly app accompanies the whole business of producing user generated content — from applying for jobs through talking to the brands to getting paid. The features implemented:

  • Browse available jobs and apply directly
  • Manage current jobs
  • Chat between brands and users
  • Upload deliverables
  • Notifications when a job changes
  • Profile management and billing overview
  • Registration and sign-in

Communication and usability tests make the difference

A clear definition of the requirements let us meet the client's wishes in a short project — and give a cost guarantee alongside it.

Continuous communication, iteration, involving the client and testing with users produced, in a short time, a full replacement for the previous web interface and a properly better experience for the people using it.
