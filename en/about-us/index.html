About us

We are thinkers and doers

ZWEIDENKER is a digital and software agency based in Cologne. Since 2009 we have been building connected digital products and helping our clients digitise the business processes they depend on.

How we started

Dr. Marcus Denker, Norbert Hartl and Christian Denker spent years working in the creative, digital and research sectors before bringing their idea of an agency to life: straightforward processes, a high bar for quality, and a way of working together built on respect and trust.

Today ZWEIDENKER is an international team of developers, strategists and designers. We work on custom software, IoT, prototyping and consulting — and for several years now on ApptiveGrid, the platform that grew out of exactly this work.

We aim for digital products and processes that have an effect and put people first. Clients can count on honesty and an open discussion culture.

How we work

Quality from concept to product

Large software projects cannot be fully planned up front — the exact requirements for going to market are rarely settled at the start. That calls for short, iterative cycles instead of a plan nobody can hold to.

Agile development

Short cycles, working results at the end of every sprint. Interdisciplinary teams in which you take an active part from day one — rather than receiving a status report.

Scrum

We ship a running system as early as possible and grow it sprint by sprint. The long-term goal holds; the route stays adjustable when requirements move.

Kanban

For coordination and self-organisation inside the team. The board makes progress visible to everyone — you included.
